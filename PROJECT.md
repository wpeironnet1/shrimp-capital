# Product brief

## Fantasy
Start with a questionable tank in a bedroom. End with an absurd global shrimp conglomerate.

## Core loop
Hatch → raise → sell → reinvest → unlock exotic species → expand tanks → prestige.

## Tone
Warm, funny, finance-literate, and self-aware. The joke is financial ambition applied to tiny crustaceans—not gambling, deceptive earnings, or pay-to-win pressure.

## Art direction
Low-resolution 2D sprites presented with modern lighting and interface polish: PS2-era nostalgia interpreted as crisp mobile pixel art, deep aquarium blues, coral red, algae green, and warm sand.

## Monetization guardrails
- No paid loot boxes in the initial plan
- No claim of real-world profit or cash-out
- No forced ads in the core session
- Boosts must be transparent and capped
- Cosmetics and season content should carry most long-term monetization

## Architecture milestones
- Local simulation and save first
- Server-authoritative economy before social competition
- Authentication and cloud save before clans
- StoreKit/Google Play through RevenueCat only after economy balance
