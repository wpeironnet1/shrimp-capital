# Product brief

## Fantasy
Start with a questionable tank in a bedroom. End with an absurd global shrimp conglomerate.

## Core loop
Hatch → raise → sell → reinvest → unlock exotic species → expand tanks → prestige.

## Tone
Warm, funny, finance-literate, and self-aware. The joke is financial ambition applied to tiny crustaceans—not gambling, deceptive earnings, or pay-to-win pressure.

## Art direction
Low-resolution 2D sprites presented with modern lighting and interface polish: PS2-era nostalgia interpreted as crisp mobile pixel art, deep aquarium blues, coral red, algae green, and warm sand.

## Interaction benchmark
Aim for the tactile richness and playful management-game energy associated with *Dave the Diver*: expressive pixel animation, lively aquarium scenes, satisfying menu transitions, responsive buttons, punchy sound cues, small celebratory effects, and visible equipment changes. This is a quality benchmark, not an asset or interface template. Shrimp Capital must use original characters, sprites, layouts, iconography, writing, music, and sound effects, with a distinct finance-comedy identity.

- Every important tap should have immediate visual, motion, haptic, and eventually audio feedback.
- Buttons should feel physical through pressed states, short transitions, and clear disabled states.
- Purchases and unlocks should visibly change the aquarium instead of only changing numbers.
- Rare shrimp should be recognizable by silhouette, size, animation, patterns, feelers, tails, crests, or claws.
- Audio should be an original cohesive set: watery ambience, soft tank bubbles, pixel clicks, cash-register flourishes, hatch pops, upgrade construction sounds, and rarity stingers.
- Respect reduced-motion, haptic, and audio settings; feedback must never be required to understand game state.

## Monetization guardrails
- No paid loot boxes in the initial plan
- No claim of real-world profit or cash-out
- No forced ads in the core session
- Boosts must be transparent and capped
- Cosmetics and season content should carry most long-term monetization

## Architecture milestones
- Local simulation and save first
- Server-authoritative economy before social competition
- Authentication and cloud save before clans
- StoreKit/Google Play through RevenueCat only after economy balance
